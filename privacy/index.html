Privacy policy

Last updated September 20, 2026.

What you type into our tools is processed in your browser and is not sent to us. The tools do not store your entries. If you press “Copy link to this result”, your entries are placed in the copied link after the # sign so the person you share it with sees the same result; that part of a link is not sent to our server, and our analytics is set up to ignore it.

Analytics, advertising and partners

Analytics. This site uses Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use it in aggregate, such as which pages are viewed and how people arrive. Google Analytics uses cookies or similar technologies and collects information such as the pages you visit, your browser and device type, and your general location. It does not receive what you type into the calculators: tool inputs are processed in your browser and are not sent to us or to Google. You can stop Google Analytics from collecting your visits on any site with the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on, available for Chrome, Firefox, Safari and Edge, or by blocking cookies in your browser settings.

Advertising and partners. At launch this site shows no advertising and has no lending, affiliate or lead-generation partners, and it does not sell personal information. Before any advertising network or partner is added, this policy will be updated to name it, describe what it collects and explain how to opt out, and any partner link will be labeled next to the link itself.
