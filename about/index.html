About Debt Payoff Math

Debt Payoff Math is a free set of debt and loan calculators that show how long payoff takes, what it costs in interest, and how each strategy compares.

This site exists to show the arithmetic behind paying off debt. Most payoff advice stops at a rule of thumb. These calculators show the month each balance is cleared, the interest paid along the way, and what changes when you pay extra, switch strategy or take a consolidation loan with a fee. The two guides then set that math beside what regulators and published research say.

The site is run by Debt Payoff Math. It is an independent educational resource. It does not lend money, arrange loans, negotiate with creditors or offer credit counseling, and nothing here is financial, legal or credit advice. Your loan agreement and statements decide your real figures.

How the content is kept honest:

  • Every calculator runs in your browser on the numbers you enter; no outside data feeds the results.
  • Each tool is checked against worked examples with known answers before any change goes live.
  • Any statistic, rate or rule in a guide links to the page it came from, with that page’s date.
  • The formulas are published on the methodology page.

Corrections. If you find a wrong figure, a broken formula or an outdated source, please write to the address on the Contact page. Confirmed errors are fixed and the page’s updated date is changed to show when.
