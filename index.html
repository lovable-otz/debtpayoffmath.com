Where the numbers come from

The calculators use no outside data. Every result is arithmetic on the balances, rates and payments you enter, with interest charged monthly at the APR ÷ 12. The guides cite named public sources, each linked and dated where it is used: the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau’s consumer answers, the Federal Trade Commission’s consumer advice on debt relief, the Federal Reserve’s G.19 Consumer Credit release for average bank rates, and peer-reviewed research such as the Journal of Marketing Research and Journal of Consumer Research studies on repayment behavior. Rate figures are checked against each new G.19 release, and cited agency pages are re-read when a guide is updated. The site is written and maintained by Debt Payoff Math. Every formula is set out on the methodology page.
