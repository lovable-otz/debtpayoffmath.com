Loan Calculator

A loan calculator finds your monthly payment, how much you can borrow, or how long payoff takes. A $25,000 loan at 8% APR over 60 months costs $506.91 a month and $5,414.59 in total interest. Change any one input and every result updates, so you can compare offers before you apply.

How is a loan payment calculated?

A fixed loan payment comes from the standard amortization formula: payment = P × r ÷ (1 − (1 + r)^−n), where P is the amount borrowed, r is the APR divided by 12, and n is the number of monthly payments. Each payment covers that month’s interest first; the rest reduces the balance.

For $25,000 at 8% over 60 months, r is 0.08 ÷ 12. The formula gives $506.91. In the first month, interest is $25,000 × 0.08 ÷ 12 = $166.67, so $340.24 goes to principal. As the balance falls, the interest share shrinks and the principal share grows, while the payment stays the same. The amortization schedule calculator shows that split year by year.

Rates vary by loan type. In the Federal Reserve G.19 release of September 8, 2026, commercial banks averaged 7.14% on 60-month new car loans and 11.86% on 24-month personal loans in the second quarter of 2026.

Enter the APR from your loan offer rather than the interest rate if they differ. The CFPB (reviewed August 28, 2026) explains that the APR is the interest rate plus additional lender fees, so it better reflects the loan’s cost. This calculator treats the APR as the rate, so it does not add fees separately.

How much can I borrow for a set payment?

Choose Amount I can borrow and the tool runs the payment formula in reverse. At 8% APR, a $500 monthly payment supports a $24,659.22 loan over 60 months. A longer term raises that amount, and a higher rate lowers it. The tool also shows the total interest you would pay.

Stretching from 60 to 72 months lets you borrow $3,858.04 more for the same payment, but adds $2,141.96 in interest.

The third mode, Time to pay off, answers a different question: how long an existing balance lasts at a given payment. $25,000 at 8% paid at $500 a month takes 62 months, with $5,511.17 in interest. If the payment does not cover the monthly interest, the tool warns you that the loan will never be repaid.

Before settling on a payment, check how it fits with your other debts using the debt-to-income ratio calculator.

How does the term change total interest?

A longer term lowers the monthly payment but raises total interest, because the balance stays outstanding longer. For $25,000 at 8%, moving from 36 to 84 months cuts the payment by more than half but more than doubles the interest. Lenders may also charge a different rate for different terms.

A middle path is to take the longer term for a lower required payment and pay extra when you can, if the loan has no prepayment penalty. Frequently asked questions

What is the monthly payment on a $25,000 loan?

At 8% APR, a $25,000 loan costs $783.41 a month over 36 months, $506.91 over 60 months, or $389.66 over 84 months. Total interest ranges from $3,202.73 to $7,731.05. A different rate changes every figure, so enter the APR from your own offer.

How long will it take to pay off my loan?

Pick Time to pay off, then enter your balance, APR and monthly payment. The tool applies each payment to that month’s interest first and counts the months until the balance reaches zero. For example, $25,000 at 8% paid at $500 a month takes 62 months and $5,511.17 in interest.

Does this loan calculator include fees?

Not separately. It uses the rate you enter, so enter the APR, which the CFPB describes as the interest rate plus additional lender fees. If a fee is taken out of the loan before you receive the money, the personal loan calculator on this site shows the monthly payment, the cash you actually get and the resulting APR.
